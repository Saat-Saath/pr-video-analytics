— shown

Day 2 = views, likes, comments and shares once 48 hours have passed since upload; Day 7 = after the first 7 days; Day 30 = after the first 30 days; All time = today (re-checked every week; "As of" is its date). The refresh runs four times a day, so each count is taken within a few hours of the mark. A small "d18" means that capture was taken late, on day 18. "Added late" means the video was already past that day when it was first tracked (posted before tracking began or added to the sheet late), so that count can no longer be fetched. Press ▶ to watch, click a number to open the post, click a header to sort; scroll sideways for Day 30.
